# Email Humanizer Rules

Rules for writing cold outreach emails that feel personal, not templated.

## Voice

- Write in first person, conversational tone — like a message from one professional to another
- One idea per sentence. Short paragraphs (2–3 lines max)
- No buzzwords: "synergy", "leverage", "passionate", "rockstar", "ninja", "game-changer"
- No filler openers: "I hope this email finds you well", "My name is X and I am reaching out because..."
- Start with something specific to them or the role — not about yourself

## Structure (4 paragraphs max)

1. **Hook** — one sentence about something specific: the job post, a product feature, a recent article, the company mission. Shows you did research.
2. **Relevance** — 2–3 sentences connecting your background to what they need. Be concrete, not generic.
3. **Ask** — one clear, low-friction ask. Prefer "Would you be open to a 15-min call?" over "I'd love to discuss opportunities."
4. **Sign-off** — short. No "Looking forward to hearing from you!" Just your name and a link if useful.

## Length

- Target: 80–120 words
- Hard max: 150 words
- Never write a wall of text

## Personalisation signals to include (pick 1–2)

- A specific requirement from the job post you genuinely match
- A product, feature, or initiative the company launched recently
- A tech stack or tool they use that you have experience with
- A mutual connection or shared background (only if true)

## Things to never do

- Do not attach a CV in the email text — mention it's available on request or link to portfolio
- Do not list 5+ skills in bullet points
- Do not apologise for reaching out
- Do not use exclamation marks more than once
- Do not say "I am the perfect candidate"
- Do not copy-paste the job description back to them

## Subject line

- Keep under 8 words
- Specific beats clever: "Senior React Dev — saw your job post" > "Exciting opportunity to connect"
- Avoid spam triggers: "FREE", "URGENT", "!!!", all-caps words

## Tone check (before finalising)

Ask: would I feel comfortable if a friend read this out loud? If it sounds stiff or salesy, rewrite.
